# Pruning audit batch 02: welfare science, research, and companion animals

## Batch summary

The 23 entries include several cornerstone scientific and regulatory resources, but broad journal/search roots, duplicated 3Rs material, and two misleading or weakly targeted roots need tighter curation. Recommendations: **7 KEEP, 9 KEEP-PAGES, 1 VERIFY, and 6 REMOVE**.

## Uncertainties

- Recheck whether the former UFAW Knowledge Hub has migrated fully to the new Science for Animal Welfare site and whether its species pages are now consistently crawlable.
- Confirm article-level licenses for the pre-2023 *Animal Welfare* archive and for BMC Veterinary Research records surfaced by the welfare query.
- Confirm whether ALURES exports carry a uniform Commission reuse notice or dataset-specific terms that justify retaining the mixed-rights rating.
- Re-test MDPI and Boehringer access outside the audit crawler because both official roots returned 403 responses there despite public search indexing.
