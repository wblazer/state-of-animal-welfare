## Uncertainties

- Substack pages were public during review, but paywall settings and platform rendering can change, and no publication-specific permissive license was found.
- ACE's current terms should be checked manually for any text/data-mining clause because its public methodology pages showed conventional site terms but no affirmative corpus license.
- We Animals applies asset-level licenses, so a parent reviewer should confirm whether catalog highlights will point only to readable reporting pages rather than imply reuse of photographs.
- `Shot on Goal` is young and its long-term maintenance and editorial rigor are not yet established, so it remains `VERIFY` despite filling a distinctive gap.
- The Animal Welfare Alignment Newsletter launched in 2026, so its strong recommendation rests on direct relevance and institutional expertise rather than a long publication record.
